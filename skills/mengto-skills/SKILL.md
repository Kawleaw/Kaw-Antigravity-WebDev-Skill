# Meng To Design & Interactive Storytelling Skills

Award-winning techniques distilled from Meng To / Design+Code for building rich, interactive, and cinematic web experiences.

## Core Workflows
1. **Interactive Storytelling**: Use pacing, scroll triggers (GSAP ScrollTrigger / Lenis / Framer Motion), and multi-layer depth to turn static pages into cinematic journeys.
2. **WebGL & Three.js 3D Elements**: Integrate lightweight 3D scenes, particle fields, or shaders that enhance without hurting performance.
3. **Video to Superprompt**: Inspect design showcase videos and reverse engineer layout, easing, and assets (see `references/video-to-superprompt/SKILL.md`).
4. **HTML to Interaction Prompts**: Break complex award-winning pages into modular reusable interaction prompts (see `references/html-to-interaction-prompts/SKILL.md`).
5. **Full-Page Stitched Capture**: Capture structure, pacing, and visual hierarchy from top to bottom (see `references/stitched-full-page-capture/SKILL.md`).
